Christmas Gift Budget Tracker (Excel and Google Sheets)

A six-tab holiday spreadsheet for gift budgets, gift exchange names, paycheck checks and spending logs. Works in Excel and Google Sheets.

For a household planning holiday gifts on a fixed budget who needs to see whether each paycheck covers the spending.

One Excel workbook with six tabs: gift list, exchange names, paycheck budget, spending log, summary and instructions.

What's included

  • Summary: totals for gift budget, gift spending, exchange budget, paycheck expenses and spending log.
  • Gift List: 5 example rows with recipient, occasion, gift idea, budget, spent, status and notes, plus a total row.
  • Gift Exchange: 5 example rows with name, drawn partner, gift budget, bought and notes, plus a total row.
  • Paycheck Budget: 6 expense rows with amount per paycheck and category, plus a total row.
  • Spending Log: 5 example purchases with date, payee, category, amount and notes, plus a total row.
  • How to use: six steps inside the workbook.

Setup steps

  1. Download the file and open it in Excel or Google Sheets.
  2. Open the How to use tab and read the six steps.
  3. On Gift List, replace the example rows with your recipients and budgets.
  4. On Paycheck Budget, enter what each paycheck sends to each expense.
  5. Record purchases on Spending Log. The Summary updates itself.

Can't edit the file?

In Google Sheets, choose File > Make a copy. In Excel, save a copy to your own drive first. The copy is yours to change.

FAQ

Does the Summary update on its own?

Yes. Gift totals, exchange budget, paycheck expenses and spending log totals feed the Summary.

Is this a bank or tax tool?

No. It is a planning sheet. It does not import bank data or send reminders.
