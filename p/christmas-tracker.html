Christmas Gift Budget Tracker (Excel and Google Sheets)

A six-tab holiday spreadsheet for gift budgets, spending logs and paycheck checks. Works in Excel and Google Sheets.

Christmas Gift Budget Tracker

For a household planning holiday gifts on a fixed budget who needs to see whether each paycheck covers the spending.

One Excel workbook with six tabs: gift list, exchange names, paycheck budget, spending log, summary and instructions.

What's included

  • Summary: totals for gift budget, gift spending, exchange budget, paycheck expenses and spending log.
  • Gift List: 5 example rows with recipient, occasion, gift idea, budget, spent, status and notes, plus a total row.
  • Gift Exchange: 5 example rows with name, drawn partner, gift budget, bought and notes, plus a total row.
  • Paycheck Budget: 6 expense rows with amount per paycheck and category, plus a total row.
  • Spending Log: 5 example purchases with date, payee, category, amount and notes, plus a total row.
  • How to use: six steps inside the workbook.
